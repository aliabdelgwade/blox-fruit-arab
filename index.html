Rules

What Can You Ban?
Macros, AHK

Macros are allowed, but only if you use approved applications (such as TGMacro) to spam keyboard keys (e.g., spamming 'R'), or dedicated keyboard software like SteelSeries GG and Razer Synapse.

AutoHotkey (AHK) is not allowed, as it has been patched since the last Blox Fruits update. Even newer variants of AHK—including third-party applications and external cheats like Scared or Pixel Trigger Suro, whether built directly on AutoHotkey or compiled as .exe files—are strictly considered cheating. If caught using any of these, you will be banned from the Blox Fruits Arab Leaderboard. If you downloaded or used them previously without knowing, delete them immediately and clear all traces before you are caught.

Scripts like Super Jump AHK are also forbidden. They interact directly with game memory and offsets, fitting the explicit definition of an external cheat.

Builds, Fruits, Races, Weapons

You cannot force a specific Build on your opponent; you do not have the right, for example, to force them to use Rocket Fruit while you play with Portal Fruit.

You cannot restrict the opponent from using a Sword, Gun, Fighting Style, or Accessories & Rings; the other player has the right to play with any Build they prefer, and you do not have the right to dictate what they use.

If they are playing with a fruit like Control and it causes frame drops or lag on your end, this is a personal issue with your device and settings, and the opponent is not responsible for it.

Spectators, Unnecessary Lag

You can remove spectators or referees; they provide no benefit to the fight and only cause unnecessary extra lag, as well as the potential for them to interfere or intentionally ruin gameplay (Trolling).

You are also allowed to do this in private servers to prevent random players in public servers from joining, ruining the match, or causing unnecessary slowdown.

Suspicious Players?
Suspicious Players, Mid Game

If a player is accused of cheating or exploiting in any way during the match (excluding the use of Macros), and the accuser provides suspicious video evidence, they have the right to request a PC Check.

If the accused player refuses to undergo the check for privacy or safety reasons, they must complete the remainder of the match while sharing their full screen (Screenshare) inside a Discord call. The accuser also has the right to request that they rejoin the match if necessary. The screenshare must display the entire screen, not just the Roblox application window.

Regarding Fast Flags: If suspicions arise about a player using settings that grant an unfair advantage (OP) and you have video clips proving so, you have the right to request that they either display their Fast Flags configuration list for review or disable all these settings for the remainder of the match.

Suspicious Players, Pre Game

If a player has a suspicious history or is known for closet cheating, they are required to share their full screen (Screenshare) during any Ranked Match. This includes starting the screen share prior to launching Roblox and keeping it active until the end of the match.

If the player wishes to clear their name and avoid mandatory screen sharing in future matches, they must pass a full PC Check. However, if suspicious video clips surface after passing the check, mandatory screen sharing will be reimposed on them for all matches.
